The site is free to use. If you'd like to help keep it going, here's how.

Free ways to help

  • Share a card. It's mostly word of mouth that brings people here.
  • Star the repo on GitHub. It helps more people find it.
  • Suggest a metric. If there's something official and important we're missing, let us know.
  • Check the numbers. If a source looks wrong or out of date, tell us.
